PayPal Pro (Payflow)

Take card payments on-site (needs a PayPal Pro account).

About this gateway

PayPal Pro (Payflow) processes card payments without sending customers to PayPal. It requires a paid PayPal Payments Pro or Payflow account. If you don't have one, use PayPal Express Checkout instead.

Not sure which one to choose?

PayPal Express Checkout (recommended): works with a standard PayPal Business account and is the right choice for almost everyone. PayPal Pro (Payflow): keeps customers on your site but needs a paid PayPal Payments Pro / Payflow account. Only choose Pro if PayPal has specifically set you up with it.
